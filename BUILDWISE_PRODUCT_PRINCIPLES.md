# BuildWise product principles

1. **Value before model choice.** Start with the business outcome, workload, risk, and constraints.
2. **Deterministic first.** Use rules, retrieval, validation, and human decisions wherever they are sufficient.
3. **Every model call has a job.** A task must state its input, output, context, model, budget, retry, fallback, and review policy.
4. **Economics are auditable.** Scenario costs are derived from explicit policies, not target savings.
5. **Evidence is labelled.** Design estimates, demo simulations, mock adapters, provider-reported usage, and verified-live results are distinct.
6. **Governance is part of the design.** Privacy, residency, approval, review, evaluation, and observability belong in the build kit.
7. **The handoff must be actionable.** Each recommendation should leave a team with an implementable next step.
